### Which should you choose?

- **For developers looking for programmatic SEO**: pSEO Engine may be the better choice due to its developer-first output via MDX integration. The absence of CMS lock-in makes it versatile for publishing to a Next.js repository.

- **For teams concerned about content quality**: pSEO Engine's features such as thin-content gating, composite quality scores, and human review queues stand out, assisting in avoiding the Google value-deficiency penalty.

- **For those needing regular performance enhancements**: The automatic provider failover in pSEO Engine enhances reliability, making it ideal for businesses that rely on consistent search visibility.
