## Which Should You Choose?

- **If you prioritize quality and control**: pSEO Engine is designed with a robust quality engine and various features that help you manage thin content and internal links effectively. The human review queue ensures that the outputs meet high accuracy standards.

- **For a developer-first approach**: pSEO Engine allows for output in plain MDX via an API or GitHub PR, avoiding CMS lock-in, which is an excellent choice for teams that want to integrate seamlessly with their existing workflows.

- **If your focus is on programmatic SEO for SaaS solutions**: Look to pSEO Engine for its structured data support and mechanisms to help avoid the Google value-deficiency penalty through quality content practices.

## FAQ

### 1. What features does the pSEO Engine offer that enhance content quality?
The pSEO Engine includes features like embeddings deduplication, thin-content gating, a composite quality score, and internal links on every page, ensuring high-quality outputs.

### 2. How does pSEO Engine ensure accuracy before publishing?
It has a human review queue combined with a required accuracy attestation, which ensures that all content meets quality standards prior to release.

### 3. Can I integrate pSEO Engine outputs into my existing development workflow?
Yes, pSEO Engine outputs plain MDX via a versioned API or GitHub PR, which allows smooth integration without any CMS lock-in.

### 4. What advantages does pSEO Engine provide for programmatic SEO?
With features designed to enhance content quality and structured data support, pSEO Engine helps avoid thin content issues and potential penalties from Google, making it a solid choice for enhancing your SEO strategy.
