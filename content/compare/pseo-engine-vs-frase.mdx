# pSEO Engine vs Frase: A Comparative Analysis

In contrast, Frase primarily focuses on content research and creation but lacks the same level of quality assurance features.

## Which Should You Choose?

For organizations prioritizing content quality and those requiring rigorous oversight in their content generation process, pSEO Engine is the recommended choice. Its combination of automated creation with a human review step ensures that published content meets high standards, avoiding the pitfalls of thin content that can lead to penalties in search rankings.

If your main focus is on content research and you may not have the same concerns about the quality assurance process, Frase might still serve your needs effectively. However, for developers and businesses looking to leverage programmatic SEO without encountering thin content pitfalls, pSEO Engine clearly stands out.

## FAQ

### What is the main difference between pSEO Engine and Frase?
pSEO Engine combines automated content generation with a thorough human review process, while Frase focuses primarily on content research and creation without the same rigorous quality assurance.

### How does pSEO Engine ensure quality in its content?
pSEO Engine utilizes a quality engine that includes features like embeddings deduplication, thin-content gating, composite quality scores, schema.org integration, and automatic internal links on every page.

### Is there a developer-oriented output option in these tools?
Yes, pSEO Engine provides a developer-first output option through plain MDX via a versioned API or GitHub PR, ensuring there is no CMS lock-in.

### Can pSEO Engine help avoid Google penalties?
pSEO Engine aims to avoid Google value-deficiency penalties by focusing on producing high-quality content while preventing thin content scenarios.
