## FAQ

**What core features does pSEO Engine offer?**  
pSEO Engine provides a high-quality engine that includes embeddings deduplication, thin-content gating, a composite quality score, schema.org support, and internal links on every page. It also has a human review queue and requires an accuracy attestation before publishing.

**How does pSEO Engine ensure quality content?**  
pSEO Engine ensures quality through its unique features, including a human review queue and composite quality scores, which focus on hedging against thin content and maintaining SEO standards.

**What is the publishing capability of pSEO Engine?**  
pSEO Engine allows users to publish programmatic pages directly to a Next.js repository, enhancing the workflow for developers with no CMS lock-in.

**Can I rely on pSEO Engine for programmatic SEO without thin content?**  
Yes, pSEO Engine is specifically designed to support programmatic SEO without generating thin content, mitigating the risk of encountering Google’s value-deficiency penalties.
