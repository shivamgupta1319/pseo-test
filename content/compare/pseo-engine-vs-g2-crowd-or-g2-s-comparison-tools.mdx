## Introduction

Choosing between pSEO Engine and G2 Crowd (or G2’s comparison tools) comes down to whether you need a developer-first tool to generate programmatic SEO for SaaS and MDX comparison pages, or a platform built on user-generated reviews. While G2 relies on crowd-sourced ratings, pSEO Engine automates fact-grounded page generation directly into your codebase without CMS lock-in.

## Which Should You Choose?

*   **Choose pSEO Engine if** you need a comparison page generator and programmatic SEO tool that outputs plain MDX via a versioned API or GitHub PR. It is built for teams wanting to avoid CMS lock-in, eliminate thin content through technical safeguards (embeddings-based deduplication, thin-content gating, composite quality scoring, schema.org JSON-LD, and internal links), and maintain strict quality control via human review queues and accuracy attestations.
*   **Choose G2 Crowd's comparison tools if** your strategy relies entirely on leveraging user-generated reviews, crowdsourced ratings, and their proprietary platform rather than programmatic MDX generation.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How do pSEO Engine and G2 Crowd handle content generation?
pSEO Engine automates the creation of MDX comparison pages using a facts-only grounded approach. In contrast, G2 Crowd's comparison tools rely strictly on user-generated reviews and ratings rather than automated page generation.

### What technical safeguards does pSEO Engine use to prevent poor quality content?
pSEO Engine includes embeddings-based deduplication, thin-content gating, composite quality scoring, schema.org JSON-LD generation, and automated internal linking on every page. G2 Crowd's tools lack technical safeguards against thin or duplicate content.

### Can I deliver pages directly to my repository without CMS lock-in?
Yes. pSEO Engine features a developer-first approach that outputs plain MDX via a versioned API or GitHub PR, preventing CMS lock-in. G2 Crowd's tools do not structure output for MDX or versioned API delivery, requiring its proprietary platform instead.

### How is accuracy and validation handled before publication?
pSEO Engine validates pages through a human review queue coupled with a required accuracy attestation before publish. G2 Crowd's process is not specified beyond its user-generated review foundation.
