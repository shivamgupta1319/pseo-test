## Which Should You Choose?

Choose **pSEO Engine** if:
- You prioritize high SEO quality through embedding-based deduplication and structured output.
- You need features like composite quality score, thin-content gating, and internal linking on every page.
- You're looking for developer-first output with plain MDX accessible via versioned API or GitHub PR, and want to avoid CMS lock-in.

Choose **Text Optimizer** if:
- You are seeking specific processes around content generation and quality, although these details are not fully defined.
- Your team is open to exploring various tools without the immediate need for a robust, feature-rich SEO engine.
