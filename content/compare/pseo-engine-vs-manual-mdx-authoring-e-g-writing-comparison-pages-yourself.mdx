## pSEO Engine vs. Manual MDX Authoring

Choosing between a programmatic SEO engine that auto‑generates MDX comparison pages and writing those pages yourself boils down to effort, quality controls, and developer workflow. pSEO Engine delivers an end‑to‑end solution that enforces a strict quality gate set—embeddings‑based duplicate detection, thin‑content filtering, a composite quality score, schema.org JSON‑LD, and mandatory internal linking—before publishing. In contrast, manual authoring relies entirely on in‑house effort, with no built‑in safeguards against thin or duplicate content.

### FAQ

**Q: Does pSEO Engine guarantee that no thin or duplicate content will be published?**  
A: It enforces thin‑content gates and embeddings‑based near‑duplicate detection, but final publication still requires a human review with an accuracy attestation.

**Q: Can I integrate pSEO Engine with my existing Next.js repo?**  
A: Yes; the engine outputs plain MDX that can be pulled via a versioned API or a GitHub PR and merged into a Next.js project.

**Q: What happens if the AI model fails during generation?**  
A: The model router automatically fails over to another provider, ensuring continuous generation without manual intervention.

**Q: Do I need to write any schema.org or internal links myself?**  
A: No. pSEO Engine automatically includes schema.org JSON‑LD and internal linking on every page it generates.
